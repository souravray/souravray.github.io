Redscoop sits in your Mac menu bar and helps you keep up with Reddit threads relevant to your interests. Get notified when a conversation matches your topics, compose a thoughtful reply with writing assistance, and participate on your own terms.

macOS only  ·  Your account, your posts

About

What it does

Surfaces relevant threads

Configure keywords and subreddits you care about. Redscoop monitors your feed in the background and notifies you when a matching thread appears, so you never miss a conversation worth joining.

Writing assistance

Compose, then you decide

A local writing assistant helps you draft a reply based on the thread context. You read it, rewrite it, and post it yourself — nothing is submitted automatically, ever.

Your account

Authenticated, not scraped

Redscoop uses Reddit's official OAuth2 API with your own account credentials. It accesses only what you have permission to see and requests only the scopes it needs.
